It is 1957, the height of the Cold War. Indiana Jones (Harrison Ford) is kidnapped by Soviet agents led by the ruthless Irina Spalko (Cate Blanchett), who need him to find a mysterious artifact. After escaping, and under suspicion from the American government, Indy accepts help from a young greaser, Mutt (Shia LaBeouf), and heads into the Peruvian jungle in search of a crystal skull and a lost city.

## New era, new mystery

If the first three films paid tribute to the adventure serials of the 1930s, this one changes decade and frame of reference. Steven Spielberg and George Lucas trade religious mysticism for the flying-saucer movies and atomic paranoia of the 1950s. The shift in tone still divides fans today.

## Ford, Blanchett and Marion

Ford proves the character is still his, and Blanchett has a blast as a villain with a thick accent. The return of Karen Allen as Marion Ravenwood brings the film's best chemistry. The jeep chase through the jungle is fun, but the excess of digital effects takes away some of the physical weight that defined the series.

## Is it worth it?

It is the weakest of Spielberg's films with the character, with moments that became internet jokes. Still, it has John Williams' score, well-choreographed action and the pleasure of seeing a classic hero again. For fans, it works as a reunion.

Bea (Sydney Sweeney) and Ben (Glen Powell) have a perfect first date that ends in a misunderstanding, and attraction turns into mutual dislike. Months later, the two find out they are going to the same wedding in Sydney: her sister is marrying his best friend's sister. To escape exes and meddling relatives, they decide to pretend they are together. You can imagine what happens.

## Shakespeare in a bikini

The script is a loose version of Shakespeare's Much Ado About Nothing, with quotations scattered through the film. Director Will Gluck, of Easy A, knows the rules of the romantic comedy and is not afraid to use every one: misunderstandings, scheming friends, a musical moment and a grand gesture at the end.

## The chemistry that holds it together

What makes the difference is the couple. Sweeney and Powell have comic timing and a tension that convinces, and the film leans on them even when the script gets silly. The Australian scenery, with the Sydney Opera House in the background, helps create a vacation mood.

## Is it worth it?

There is nothing new here, and some jokes are forced. But Anyone But You became an unexpected hit that filled theaters by word of mouth and reminded Hollywood that audiences still like romantic comedies. Ideal for watching with a date, without big expectations.

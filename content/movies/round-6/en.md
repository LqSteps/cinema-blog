Seong Gi-hun (Lee Jung-jae) is a divorced father, addicted to gambling and drowning in debt. One day a stranger invites him to a game with a prize of 45.6 billion won. He accepts and wakes up on an island with 455 other contestants as desperate as he is, all in green tracksuits. The games are childhood games, like Red Light, Green Light. Whoever loses, dies.

## An idea rejected for ten years

Creator Hwang Dong-hyuk wrote the story more than a decade before it premiered and was told by everyone that it was too absurd. When the show reached Netflix in 2021, it became the platform's most-watched series to date, and the whole world learned to recognize the guards' pink jumpsuits.

## The game is inequality

Beneath the violence, Squid Game is about money and about who gets pushed to the edge of the abyss. Characters like Sae-byeok, Sang-woo and old Il-nam put a face on that desperation, and that's what makes every elimination hurt.

## Is it worth it?

The first season earned Lee Jung-jae and Hwang historic Emmys, the first ever for a non-English-language series. The second and third seasons, released in 2024 and 2025, close the story. Watch it in the original Korean, with subtitles.

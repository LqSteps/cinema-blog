Thanos (Josh Brolin) wants to gather the six Infinity Stones, artifacts that give whoever wields them absolute power over the universe. With the Avengers divided since Captain America: Civil War, Earth's heroes must join forces with the Guardians of the Galaxy, Doctor Strange and Black Panther to stop him, scattered across New York, Wakanda, Scotland and distant planets.

## Thanos's journey

The great achievement of Anthony and Joe Russo is telling the story from the villain's point of view. Thanos is not a monster without reason: with the calm of a fanatic, he believes he is saving the universe. Brolin, through motion capture, gives the character weight and even sadness, and his relationship with his adopted daughter, Gamora (Zoe Saldaña), is the emotional heart of the film.

## A puzzle of heroes

Bringing dozens of characters together without the film turning into a mess is an achievement. The script creates unexpected and fun pairings, like Thor (Chris Hemsworth) and Rocket, or Iron Man (Robert Downey Jr.) and Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch), and alternates between storylines with rhythm. The battle in Wakanda is grand, but the clash on Titan is the one that impresses most.

## Is it worth it?

Infinity War requires knowing at least the main earlier Marvel films, and it does not truly end: the conclusion came in Avengers: Endgame. But few franchise films have dared so much. The ending, which we won't spoil, left audiences around the world in silence.

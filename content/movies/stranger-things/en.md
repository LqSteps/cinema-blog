Hawkins, Indiana, November 1983. Will Byers vanishes while biking home, and his friends Mike, Dustin and Lucas set out to find him. Along the way they meet Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown), a girl with a shaved head and strange powers who has escaped from a government lab hiding the gateway to a place called the Upside Down.

## Built from references, with a soul of its own

Brothers Matt and Ross Duffer mix Spielberg, Stephen King and John Carpenter without hiding their influences. What holds it all together, though, is the kids' friendship and performances like Winona Ryder's as the desperate mother and David Harbour's as Sheriff Hopper.

## Synthesizers and Kate Bush

Kyle Dixon and Michael Stein's electronic score became the show's signature. And in the fourth season, a scene set to "Running Up That Hill" sent Kate Bush's song back up the charts worldwide, nearly forty years after its release.

## Is it worth it?

The seasons grew longer and grander, and not always better. Even so, the first season is close to perfect, and watching this group grow up all the way to the 2025 finale carries an emotional weight that's hard to match.

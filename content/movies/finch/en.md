A solar flare has destroyed the ozone layer and turned Earth into a scorching desert. Finch Weinberg (Tom Hanks), a robotics engineer, has survived for years in an underground lab in St. Louis, with only his dog Goodyear for company. Sick and knowing his time is short, he builds a robot to look after the dog once he is gone. With a giant storm closing in, the three hit the road for San Francisco.

## A robot learning to be human

The film's great character is the robot, eventually named Jeff. Played by Caleb Landry Jones through motion capture and voice, he starts out as clumsy as a child and learns, over the course of the trip, what it means to trust and to care. His relationship with the dog, who rejects him at first, is both funny and moving.

## Hanks in Cast Away mode

As in Cast Away, Hanks carries the film almost alone, acting opposite someone who can't answer back in kind. He makes Finch grumpy, frightened and tender, and gives the predictable script a truth it wouldn't have with another actor. Director Miguel Sapochnik, who came from Game of Thrones episodes, finds beautiful images in the desert.

## Is it worth it?

Finch doesn't surprise, and its pace is too calm for anyone expecting action. But it's a gentle road movie, good for family viewing, and proof that Hanks can move you even next to a tin can. It was released straight to Apple TV+.

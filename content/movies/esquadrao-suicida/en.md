In a world that has just lost Superman, government agent Amanda Waller (Viola Davis) has a risky idea: assemble a team of imprisoned supervillains to face threats no ordinary human could. Among them are the elite marksman Deadshot (Will Smith), the unpredictable Harley Quinn (Margot Robbie), the pyrokinetic El Diablo and the brute Killer Croc. A bomb implanted in each neck makes sure no one runs.

## Harley Quinn steals the show

The best thing about Suicide Squad is Margot Robbie. Her Harley Quinn is funny, dangerous and sad, and every scene with her has an energy the rest of the film lacks. Will Smith also works as the hitman who is a devoted father, and Viola Davis frightens without raising her voice. Jared Leto's much-advertised Joker, on the other hand, barely appears.

## A confused edit

The first half hour, with music-video-paced introductions and rock hits in a row, is the most fun. After that the story falls apart: the final threat is made of uninspired digital effects, and the tone swings between grim and flippant without ever settling. Behind-the-scenes reports talk of several versions of the cut, and you can feel it.

## Is it worth it?

Suicide Squad made a lot of money and won the Oscar for makeup, but it is one of DC's most uneven films. It is worth it for Harley Quinn, who later got a film of her own. In 2021, James Gunn made a new version, The Suicide Squad, which is a lot more fun.

The world is freezing over and every animal is migrating south. Every animal, that is, except Manny, a lonely mammoth who prefers to walk the other way. Along the road he picks up the unwanted company of Sid, a sloth who can't keep quiet, and the two find a lost human baby. They decide to return him to his tribe, guided by Diego, a saber-toothed tiger who offers to help with suspicious kindness.

## A squirrel and an acorn

Before any of the main characters appear, the film introduces Scrat, the prehistoric squirrel obsessed with an acorn. His dialogue-free misadventures, which interrupt the plot every now and then, became the series' trademark and are pure silent cinema, with the timing of classic cartoons.

## More heart than it seems

Behind the jokes, Ice Age is a story about grief and about choosing your own family. A scene in a cave of rock paintings reveals Manny's past without a single line of dialogue and shows that Blue Sky Studios knew how to move audiences as well as make them laugh. Brazilian co-director Carlos Saldanha would go on to helm two of the sequels and Rio.

## Is it worth watching?

The animation has aged a little by today's standards, but the lean script, at just over 80 minutes, still works for all ages. And the Brazilian dub became so iconic that many people can't imagine Sid with any other voice.

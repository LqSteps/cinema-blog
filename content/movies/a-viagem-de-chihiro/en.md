Chihiro is ten, moving house and in a bad mood. On the way to their new home, the family stops at an abandoned theme park, her parents eat food they shouldn't and turn into pigs. To save them, the girl has to work at a bathhouse for spirits run by the witch Yubaba.

## A world that doesn't explain its rules

Miyazaki never pauses the story to teach you how this universe works. Chihiro, and the audience with her, learns it all by doing: the contract that steals names, Kamaji's boiler fire, the mysterious No-Face. The strangeness never turns into confusion, because every creature has weight and personality.

## Hand-drawn animation

Every frame of the bathhouse feels alive, with dozens of characters moving at once. Joe Hisaishi's score follows the girl from loneliness to courage without ever overdoing it.

## Is it worth it?

Winner of the Golden Bear in Berlin and the Oscar for Best Animated Feature, Spirited Away was for almost twenty years the highest-grossing film in Japanese history. It works for children and adults alike, each discovering a different film.
